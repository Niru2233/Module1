Japan Tour


Japan is a mix of ancient traditions and ultra-modern city life, making it one of the world's most popular travel destinations.


Popular Destinations

  • Tokyo

  • Tokyo I'd suggest kicking off your trip here. It is a thrilling blend of futuristic skyscrapers and quiet, historic pockets.
  • The Vibe: Neon lights, bustling intersections, and endless dining options.
  • Don't Miss: The famous scramble crossing in Shibuya, the historic Sensō-ji Temple, and the electronics and anime capital of Akihabara.

  • Kyoto

  • Kyoto This is Japan's cultural heart and a complete contrast to Tokyo. If you want to see traditional wooden machiya houses and peaceful gardens, this is the place.
  • The Vibe: Traditional, serene, and steeped in history.
  • Don't Miss: Walking through the thousands of vermilion gates at Fushimi Inari Taisha, visiting the stunning Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion), and exploring the historic streets of Gion.

  • About Company

The global travel industry is built on surprising corporate reinventions, where Thomas Cook practically invented modern group tourism in 1841 with a simple 11-mile train charter for a temperance rally, decades before American Express transformed from an 1850s horseback express mail service into a luxury travel card empire. In the digital age, tech disruption took over when Expedia originated as a small internal software experiment inside Microsoft in 1996, while the cash-strapped founders of Airbnb kept their early startup alive by designing and selling custom presidential-themed cereal boxes for $40 each to fund their concept. Today, the scale of global leisure is massive, highlighted by companies like Royal Caribbean operating floating cities like the Icon of the Seas, which stands as the largest cruise ship ever built, holding up to 7,600 passengers.
