Data analysis

Data analysis is the systematic process of collecting, cleaning, transforming, and interpreting raw data to discover useful insights, answer questions, and support decision-making.

Key Types of Data Analysis


Descriptive Analysis: Summarizes past historical data to answer what happened.
Diagnostic Analysis: Explores data deeper to find the root cause of past events, answering why it happened.
Predictive Analysis: Uses historical data and statistical models to forecast what is likely to happen.
Prescriptive Analysis: Recommends specific actions and data-driven strategies to determine what should be done next.
